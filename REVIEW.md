Also note anything you looked at and decided was **not** a defect, and why:
1. **Removing `_id` via `doc.pop("_id", None)`**: MongoDB's internal `_id` is an implementation detail that must never appear in API responses according to `DATA_MODEL.md` and `openapi.yaml`.
2. **Addressing attendance records by `(emp_code, date)`**: The specification explicitly states attendance records have no surrogate ID and are naturally addressed by the compound key `(emp_code, date)`.
3. **Storing dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`) and shift times (`HH:MM`) as strings in MongoDB**: `DATA_MODEL.md` specifies that dates and shift times are intentionally strings to avoid timezone drift on pure calendar values.
